# Rung 6 · human-in-the-loop {{todo|NOT STARTED}}

**Cost measured in human minutes, never converted to currency.**

Specified below so it can be built without re-deriving the contract. See [[contributing]].

## Role

- A person settles what the ladder could not.
- Runs last. Everything still open after [[r5]] is what reaches a human.

## It is a rung, not a prompt

- "Tell the model to escalate when unsure" is [[r5]], not rung 6. **Rung 6 is a person.**

## Settings

`manifest.rungs.6`

| Setting | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `mode` | `simulated` | costs the queue without anyone working it |
| `minutes_per_record` | 2.0 | the assumed cost per review |

- A `timed` mode would measure real minutes. Until then `2.0` is an **assumption, not a measurement**, and any figure derived from it inherits that.

## What a person is shown

- The record, and `checks["withheld"]` — what the system was going to say before [[r5]] withdrew it.
- Abstention is a withdrawal, never a deletion, **precisely so this rung has something to show**.
- The [[r1]] verdict and reason, so the reviewer knows why it stalled.

## What to write

- `rec.mark(6, ZONE_RESOLVED)` when settled, or `ZONE_ESCALATE` when queued but not yet reviewed.
- The two are different states and the [[ledger]] must distinguish them, or "sent to a person" and "a person answered" become the same number.

## Ledger

One row per record, `rung=6`: `human_minutes` set (zero token cost), `outcome="escalated"` or `"settled"`. Surfaced as `reviews_per_100`.

## The measure

- `human_minutes` is the **third currency** and is never fused with tokens or latency.
- The question the project exists to ask is *would you rather spend tokens or human attention* — fusing them deletes the question.
- Rung 6 is where the ladder stops being cheap, so the marginal figure here is the one that decides where a real deployment stops.

## How you will know it works

- On the gold control, everything reaching rung 6 is a **correct answer the ladder failed to confirm** — 222 of 393 on the test split. That is the review queue a perfect extractor still generates, and it is the strongest single argument in the project.
